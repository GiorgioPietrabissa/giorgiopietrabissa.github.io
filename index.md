I'm an Assistant Professor at the Department of Geography and Environment at the [LSE](https://www.lse.ac.uk/people/giorgio-pietrabissa). I'm an associate at the Centre for Economic Performance ([CEP](https://cep.lse.ac.uk/_new/people/person.asp?id=11524)) and a fellow of the Fondazione Rodolfo Debenedetti. I'm also a Visiting Scholar at Humboldt University. I received my PhD from CEMFI in 2024.

My main research interests lie in urban and public economics. I use quantitative empirical methods to understand how public policies affect people's quality of life across and within cities.

I'm an Assistant Professor at the Department of Geography and Environment at the LSE. I am an associate at the Centre for Economic Performance (CEP) and a fellow of the Fondazione Rodolfo Debenedetti. I'm also a Visiting Scholar at Humboldt University. I received my PhD from CEMFI in 2024.

My main research interests lie in urban and public economics. I use quantitative empirical methods to understand how public policies improve the quality of life of people across places.

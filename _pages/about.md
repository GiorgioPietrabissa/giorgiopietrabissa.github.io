I’m an Assistant Professor at the **Department of Geography and Environment** at the **LSE**. \
I am an associate at the Center for Economic Performance (CEP) and a fellow of the Fondazione Rodolfo Debenedetti. I'm also a Visiting Scholar at Humboldt University. \
I received my PhD from CEMFI in 2024. You can find my CV <a href="https://giorgiopietrabissa.github.io/files/CV.pdf" target="_blank">here</a>.

My research interests lie in urban, international and public economics, with a specific focus on inequality. I use quantitative empirical methods to understand how public policies affect economic outcomes across space and shape spatial inequalities.
